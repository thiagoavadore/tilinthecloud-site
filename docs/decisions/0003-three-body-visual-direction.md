# 0003: "Three-Body System" visual direction

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-06
- **Deciders:** thiago, claude
- **Relates to:** 0005, 0007

## Context and problem statement

The site must read as credible to senior technical buyers (CTO, VP Eng, Head of Architecture),
never as a futurist, and must avoid generic AI-slop aesthetics. The positioning is "The
Three-Body Program": one operator, three bodies (architecture, teams, AI capability) moving
together. Three directions were mocked up and put to Thiago.

## Considered options

- **Three-Body System** (chosen): ink ground, molten copper accent, an animated three-body
  orbital mark that turns the thesis into the visual system.
- **Operator's Console**: dark + monospace, truest to the "still writes the code" persona, but
  carries a gimmick / terminal-LARP risk.
- **Recovering CTO (Editorial)**: warm serif, calm and safe, but leans "writer" over "operator"
  and is a weaker consulting brand.

## Decision

Build the Three-Body System direction. The orbital mark (one copper core, three orbiting
bodies) recurs in the favicon, header, and hero.

## Consequences

- **Good:** an ownable mark built straight from the thesis; distinctive but sober; on-brand.
- **Cost / risk:** the bespoke motif must stay subtle or it reads "agency"; more hand-written
  CSS and a load animation than an off-the-shelf template.
- **Follow-ups:** typography choice in 0005; motif kept quiet, reduced-motion respected.
