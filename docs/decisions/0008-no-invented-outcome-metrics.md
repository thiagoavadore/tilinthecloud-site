# 0008: No invented outcome metrics on the proof strip

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-06
- **Deciders:** thiago, claude
- **Relates to:** BACKLOG.md T23

## Context and problem statement

The brief asks that every claim anchor to something hands-on "with a number on the outcome." At
the same time, CLAUDE.md forbids naming two specific former clients anywhere, and the only
nameable proof is GRESB, Nike, LINKIT, and "as a CTO". We do not have approved, publishable
outcome numbers yet.

## Considered options

- **Ship named credibility only, no fabricated numbers** (chosen)
- Invent plausible-sounding metrics to satisfy the "with a number" goal
- Cite numbers tied to the confidential clients

## Decision

The proof strip uses only the nameable credibility (GRESB, Nike, LINKIT, "as a CTO") plus the
real "twenty years from statistician to CTO" line. No metric is published until Thiago supplies
real, approved figures.

## Consequences

- **Good:** honest, and safe against the confidentiality rule.
- **Cost / risk:** the proof reads softer than the "with a number" ideal until real figures land.
- **Follow-ups:** BACKLOG T23, get publishable numbers (team size, modules migrated,
  time-to-first-AI-feature, or similar) and work them in.
